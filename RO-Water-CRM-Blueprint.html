1. Executive Summary

This blueprint defines a single, unified platform that combines a customer-facing RO water service website with an internal AI-powered CRM. It replaces fragmented cold-calling spreadsheets and scattered online leads with one automated pipeline: every lead — whether uploaded by the team or captured from digital marketing — is enriched, called by an AI voice agent, qualified, filtered into the correct segment, and routed to a human only when the customer asks for one or as needed.

1Unified Lead Panel
24×7AI Telecalling
100%Automatic Follow-ups

The Problem Today

  • Cold-calling data lives in spreadsheets; online leads live in ad dashboards — no single source of truth.
  • Human telecallers are expensive, inconsistent, and cannot scale past a few hundred calls a day.
  • Leads go cold because follow-ups are manual and forgettable.
  • No reliable qualification/filtering; sales agents waste time on unqualified contacts.
  • No visibility into which channel produces real, paying customers.

The Solution

A Next.js + PostgreSQL + Prisma platform with a Twilio + OpenAI GPT-4o Realtime voice engine that autonomously dials leads, holds natural conversations, qualifies intent (new install, AMC, repair, filter/part replacement), answers FAQs, detects a request for a human, and writes every outcome back into a single CRM database.

Core promise: The owner sees every cold-call and online lead in one panel, fully automated by AI, with clean segmentation and an instant human escalation path — no lead is ever lost.

8. Module 4 — AI Telecalling Engine

Autonomous outbound + inbound voice automation built on Twilio Programmable Voice and the OpenAI GPT-4o Realtime API.

Core Capabilities

  • Auto-dialing: Preview, Progressive & Predictive modes with configurable concurrency per campaign.
  • Natural conversation: Realtime speech-to-speech, barge-in, natural pauses, dialect-aware.
  • Multilingual: Hindi, English, Hinglish (extensible to regional languages).
  • Qualification engine: Asks scripted + dynamic questions, extracts intent, budget, location, RO type, urgency.
  • Knowledge base (RAG): Answers pricing, AMC terms, service areas from an updatable knowledge base.
  • Objection handling: Trained responses to "not interested", "too costly", "already have RO".
  • Human detection: Recognizes "talk to a person / human / agent" keywords & sentiment → triggers hand-off.
  • Compliance guardrails: Honest AI disclosure, DND/TRAI checks, call-recording consent.
  • Auto follow-up: No-answer/busy → scheduled retries; positive → confirmation SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Post-call AI: Transcription → summary → sentiment → score → next best action.

9. AI Voice Agent — Call Flow & Sample Scripts

1
Greeting & disclosure: "Namaste, main [Company] se ek AI assistant hoon…"
2
Purpose: Confirm interest & qualify need (new RO, service, AMC, repair).
3
Qualify: RO present? Brand age? Problem? City/area? Best time?
4
Handle objections & present relevant offer / plan.
5
Detect human request → transferToHuman or callback ticket.
6
Close/next step: Book service, send quote on WhatsApp, or schedule follow-up.
7
Wrap-up: Confirm details, thank, log outcome & score in CRM.

AI: Namaste! Main [Company] se ek AI assistant hoon. Aapke area mein
    RO water purifier service ke liye call kar raha hoon. 2 minute hai?
Customer: Haan boliye.
AI: Bahut badhiya. Kya ghar ya office mein RO purifier lagana chahte hain,
    ya existing purifier ki servicing karani hai?
Customer: Servicing karani hai.
AI: Theek hai. Aapka purifier kitne saal purana hai aur last service
    kab hui thi?
Customer: 2 saal, 6 mahine pehle.
AI: Samajh gaya. Filters change + general service recommend karta hoon.
    Aap kaunse area se hain, taaki main nearest technician confirm kar saku?
Customer: [Area]

    Kal shaam 5 baje ka slot available hai — chalega?

11. Human Hand-off Mechanism

When a customer asks for a human (or an edge case is detected), the system escalates instantly.

Path A — Live Warm Transfer
  • AI detects trigger phrase / sentiment
  • Looks up available agent (skill + language + area)
  • Uses Twilio <Dial> to bridge the call
  • Passes a spoken/written context summary to agent
  • Target: < 15s, call not dropped
Path B — Callback Ticket
  • No agent available / outside hours
  • AI collects preferred time & reason
  • Creates high-priority ticket, assigns to agent
  • Sends confirmation SMS/WhatsApp to customer
  • Agent calls back within SLA

Trigger Phrases (configurable)

"talk to human", "agent se baat", "real person", "manager", "insaan", "customer care", "kisi aadmi se", sentiment = frustrated/angry, or lead score ≥ 90.

15. Security, Compliance & DND (TRAI)

  • TRAI/DND compliance: scrub numbers against DND registry; honor consent flags; calling-hour restrictions.
  • AI disclosure: agent transparently identifies as AI (configurable by region).
  • Call recording consent: announcement + configurable opt-in.
  • Data protection: DPDP Act (India) alignment — encryption at rest/in transit, data retention policy, PII masking.
  • Access control: RBAC, 2FA for admins, audit logs of every lead/call action.
  • Secrets & keys: Twilio/OpenAI keys in a secrets manager; webhook signature verification.
  • Backups: automated daily DB backups + point-in-time recovery.
Note: Outbound calling regulations vary by region. Final calling strategy (consent, timing, disclosure) will be confirmed with legal/compliance before go-live.
